# Risk Engine

The risk engine must run before every order in Backtest, Paper and Live.

## Checks
Daily loss limit, daily profit target, max trades, max open positions, max quantity, max capital deployed, strategy allocation, consecutive losses, account drawdown, time restrictions, instrument restrictions, strategy status, broker connection and market data health.

## Kill Switch
Exit positions, cancel pending orders, pause live strategies, block new entries and activate broker kill switch where available.
